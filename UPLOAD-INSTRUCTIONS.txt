3. It attempts to begin playback automatically.
4. The activation tap primes the YouTube player to improve autoplay.
5. If a phone still enforces its own autoplay restriction, a small
   Tap to Start Audio fallback appears only after the automatic attempt.
6. The embedded fullscreen button is disabled.
